# Figure 4

The portal provides a modernized webform (VA10091) to establish or update VA vendor records.
Vendors must set up an account with the VA to get paid for goods or services.
The Claim/Invoice Status section serves as a reconciliation tool for providers and vendors to verify claim status and run payment reports.
Access requests are accepted from SAM.gov registered companies (System for Award Management).
The contact phone number listed is 1-512-460-5380.
